Owner Agent Studio

Design a personal Muse agent that runs your routine work like a chief of staff, inside approval rules you set, and gives you hours of your week back. You bring the judgment. The agent brings the hours.

Start with you, and where your week goes

A personal agent is only worth having if it is aimed at the work that actually eats your week. Name the leaks first. The agent plan is built from them.

You name your agent in the Soul step, where its identity, values, and voice are set. A persona based default is filled in if you leave it blank.

Tick each drain that applies and estimate your hours per week honestly. The plan targets these hours first.

The sample is a fictional owner (Jordan Meyer, founder of a fictional real estate firm), included so you can test the studio in one click.

Choose how your agent works

Persona, tone, and proactivity are not cosmetics. They decide what lands in front of you, what gets handled quietly, and what never happens without your word. Every choice writes itself into the agent charter on the right.

Set the approval matrix, then name the owner

    
This is the governance step, and it is the whole product. For each kind of action, decide what the agent may do on its own, what it may only draft for your approval, and what it must never do. The defaults below are deliberately conservative. Loosen them later, by charter amendment, once trust is earned.

Final step: create your agent in Muse

      
Copy the final output below and paste it into Muse. Your personal agent is created from this package. The package holds everything Muse needs, in three sections: who your agent is (its soul: name, identity, character, values, never-do rules, and voice), what your agent does (the system prompt, your time leak plan, the first week starter tasks, and a sample morning brief), and where you stay in charge (the full AGENT Framework charter with the approval matrix and standing rules, and your owner profile).
